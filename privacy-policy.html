Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to BackseBacho Study Portal (accessible via mjpru.online). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and secure your information when you browse our portal, fill out student forms, or buy premium study resources.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better experience and deliver study materials effectively:

  • Form & Lead Data: When you purchase material or submit a request, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp Number, Course/Branch, and Academic Year. This data is processed via third-party form handlers (Formspree).
  • Transactional Information: For premium materials (Notes, PYQs, and Bundles), payments are made securely via third-party payment links (Razorpay). We do not collect, view, or store your credit/debit card details, net banking credentials, or UPI details on our servers.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Like general web portals, we track standard browser types, IP addresses, reference pages, and timestamp sequences to audit traffic health and enhance platform interface.

2. Third-Party Analytics Tools

We track user behavior patterns anonymously to scale resource setups. Our setup leverages:

  • Google Analytics (gtag.js): Tracks general geographic metrics, duration counters, and path layouts.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: Optimizes script payloads and asset speeds based on user device patterns.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our web pages. Please note the following regarding ad delivery:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

The shared details are strictly utilized to:

  • Process material requests and verify structural delivery status via email/WhatsApp support.
  • Maintain site integrity and prevent spam attempts across checkout nodes.
  • Optimize educational product lists according to specific branch selections.

Note: We do not sell, lease, lease-trade, or distribute student records or contact details to third-party marketing networks.

University Affiliation Disclaimer: BackseBacho is an independent educational portal built exclusively to help students with notes and previous year papers. This portal is NOT officially affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University (MJPRU), Bareilly.

5. Data Security

Your connection to our portal is secured using standard HTTPS encryption layers. While we take maximum precautions to safeguard data flowing through our integrated systems, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute absolute security.
